# Credits

VR Bench stands on other people's work. One line per person or project, name first, then what they gave.

- elliotttate - OpenXR Simulator (https://github.com/elliotttate/OpenXR-Simulator), the headset-free runtime
  this is built for, and OpenXR-Layer-OBSMirror (https://github.com/elliotttate/OpenXR-Layer-OBSMirror), the
  layer that lets OBS record the real headset view. VR Bench is made with the knowledge gained from using his
  tools.
- webhead2oo9 - the OpenXR Simulator fork with the 32-bit runtime, the command files and the MCP helper
  (https://github.com/webhead2oo9/OpenXR-Simulator).
- mbucchia - OpenXR-Layer-Template (https://github.com/mbucchia/OpenXR-Layer-Template), the shape the layer
  follows.
- The Khronos Group - the OpenXR headers in third_party/openxr (Apache-2.0 OR MIT).
- OBS Studio (https://obsproject.com) and exeldro's Source Record plugin, used for the recordings.

If you should be credited here and are not, email us and we will fix it as soon as we can. We honour
correction and removal requests from rights holders.
